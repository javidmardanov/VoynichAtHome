# Review of the Voynich@Home acceptance rule

**Verdict: changes requested before statistical registration.** Keep the median-distance score for exploration. Replace its calibration before claiming a controlled probability of rejecting a true generator. An empty adaptive search supports a statement about evaluated settings, not exclusion of the continuous parameter domain.

Reviewed branch `claude/voynich-at-home-sotqwg`, commit [`f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5`](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/commit/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5). This review answers the four requests in order. It covers the request, `CALIBRATION.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`, committed calibration JSON, and the relevant Rust implementation. It is a statistical and implementation review, not verification that the implemented generators faithfully reproduce the published historical hypotheses.

## 1. Is rule C defensible, and what should change?

**As a frozen descriptive screen: yes. As a calibrated statistical compatibility test with approximately 1% false rejection: not as written.**

The smallest defensible change is to call the result “passed the registered distance screen,” remove the 1% guarantee, disclose the calibration's limitations, and restrict negative conclusions to the exact settings evaluated. This preserves the current computation but substantially limits its interpretation. For statistical compatibility language, I recommend the replacement below.

### Why the current calibration does not establish its stated error rate

1. **It conditions on one lucky or unlucky pseudo-manuscript.** The 64 distances vary the comparison seed while holding the planted target, its fingerprint, and its estimated scales fixed. Repeated experiments would also vary that target. A new manuscript generated by the same parameters can have a different location, scale, or mode. That variation is missing from the calibration.
2. **It transfers between parameter settings without a justification.** Nearby settings already have very different self-distance distributions. A threshold calibrated at one setting need not have the same coverage at another. Pooling five settings provides neither pointwise nor worst-case coverage over a family.
3. **The 2,000 draws do not supply 2,000 independent generator experiments.** They reuse a pool of 64 simulations. Without-replacement subsets provide an estimate of batch behavior, but their conditional finite-pool quantile is not an exact prediction threshold for a fresh independent batch. Subsampling itself is not necessarily invalid: averaging over fresh pools can estimate the desired batch distribution. The missing step is uncertainty and coverage for the estimated quantile. Increasing the number of subset draws does not resolve uncertainty about the generator distribution or the target. See [`subset_median_quantile`](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-core/src/calib.rs#L180).

A simple counterexample isolates the first problem even with perfect calibration: suppose a true generator produces a corpus summary of 0 with probability 0.9 and 100 with probability 0.1. Use unit scale and eight comparison replicates. Conditional on a planted target of 0, the exact 99th percentile of the median distance is 0. Applying this threshold to fresh true targets rejects **10.447875%** of them. This is an analytic illustration, **not an estimate of the self-citation generator's error rate**. The accompanying audit script computes the binomial probabilities exactly up to floating-point arithmetic.

### Recommended replacement: calibrate across pseudo-targets at each tested setting

Keep the generator, summaries, and median. Replace the family-wide epsilon calculation with a Monte Carlo rank test of a fully specified setting. Use this for the final evaluation of a list of settings frozen before evaluating confirmation text.

Let `A(X)` be the registered target-building algorithm applied to corpus `X`: fingerprint, scales, weights, and optional covariance. Freeze its resampling count, randomness policy, floors, and all other options. Let `G(theta | L, D)` be the fully specified generator at setting `theta`, conditional on the declared layout `L` and any permitted frozen development resources `D`.

Proposed initial constants: **N = 16**, **B = 999**, **alpha = 0.01**. These are practical starting choices; N concerns discrimination and Monte Carlo stability, while B determines rank resolution.

For each frozen setting:

1. Generate a reference panel `Y[1..N]` independently from `G(theta | L, D)`. Freeze this panel before inspecting the confirmation fingerprint.
2. Define `T_theta(X) = median_r distance(fingerprint(Y[r]), A(X))`.
3. Generate **B independent pseudo-target corpora** `X[1..B]` from that same generator and layout, independently of the reference panel. Rebuild `A(X[b])` for each. Evaluate `T[b] = T_theta(X[b])` and `T_obs = T_theta(X_observed)` identically.
4. Compute `p_theta = (1 + count(T[b] >= T_obs)) / (B + 1)`.
5. **Reject the setting when `p_theta <= 0.01`; otherwise retain it as “not rejected by the registered summaries.”** With B = 999, rejection requires at most nine simulated scores at least as large as the observed score. Count ties with `>=`; do not report p = 0.

The common reference panel saves simulations: it needs N + B generated corpora per setting, rather than N new comparisons for every pseudo-target. Rebuilding the target still costs computation and should be benchmarked before registering the budget.

Conditional on the independent reference panel and permitted fixed information, the observed and simulated scores are exchangeable under a fully specified true generator. Their rank therefore gives a test with false rejection probability at most alpha, including conservative treatment of ties. This is the standard Monte Carlo testing argument; the particular median/target-builder construction above is my proposed application. See [Dufour, Monte Carlo tests with nuisance parameters, sections 2–4](https://jeanmariedufour.github.io/Dufour_1995_MCT_W.pdf).

**Conditions that must hold:** all unknown generator parameters and initial-state rules are fixed or handled explicitly; resources are not fitted only to the observed confirmation text; observed and simulated corpora have matching selection and layout rules; and the scoring rule and candidate list have not been selected using the confirmation scores. For a path-dependent process, simulate the declared conditional continuation or reset model. A freshly restarted short corpus is not automatically distributed like selected quires of a longer trajectory.

If confirmation text is used to fit generator parameters or resources, freezing its fitted values and generating from them is a plug-in bootstrap, not this exact test. Either keep that fitting on development data, justify an appropriate conditional test, or explicitly design and calibrate the fitted procedure. Merely repeating the fit in a bootstrap does not automatically give finite-sample validity.

The guarantee concerns repeated manuscripts from the specified generator and the simulation procedure. It is not a probability that a generator is historically correct, or an assurance of power against every wrong generator.

**This is a design for a valid future evaluation, not a retroactive repair of data exposure.** The whole-corpus tuning disclosed in section 4A must be audited before treating the current confirmation evaluation as independent of method selection. Unless that adaptation can be validly accounted for, retain descriptive status for the current manuscript experiment even after implementing the improved test; prospective synthetic validation can still be genuinely sequestered.

## 2. Instructions for each of the seven judgment calls

### 1. The percentile and median

**Median: fine as a registered discrepancy. Current percentile calibration: replace it.** Use the rank rule above for inference. The heavy tail is part of the model, not evidence that Wilson intervals themselves fail; rule A's weakness here is the chosen tolerance and decision objective.

Publish median, upper-tail quantiles, and counts of degenerate outputs. A median intentionally suppresses minority bad runs; that behavior needs to be visible. Keep calibration, search, controls, and confirmation on their declared N values. Do not reuse an N = 8 threshold for N = 16.

The numerical audit exactly enumerates the finite-pool median distribution from the committed 64 distances. Its 99th percentiles are:

| Planted target | N = 8 | N = 16 |
|---|---:|---:|
| On-grid | 4.990642 | 3.221819 |
| Off-grid | 2.310379 | 2.164929 |

These are finite-pool calculations, not corrected population thresholds. The reported 2,000-draw estimates, 5.039490 and 2.314198, are reasonably close to their N = 8 finite-pool values. The fundamental problem is what is being calibrated, rather than an obvious failure to approximate that particular quantile.

### 2. Per-family thresholds and high-variance generators

**Use calibration conditional on the tested setting; do not impose an arbitrary cap from manuscript resampling.** A diffuse generator can legitimately be difficult to reject. Tightening its threshold until it fails changes the error guarantee.

If the scientific objective also requires a generator to be useful or predict accurately, add a separately named adequacy requirement with tolerances justified in raw statistic units. Register that objective and measure its operating characteristics separately. Do not describe the combined rejection rule as a 1%-level goodness-of-fit test without calibrating the combination.

Under the recommended rule, report which registered alternatives the screen can distinguish. Retained high-variance settings may remain unresolved because the fingerprint has little power against them.

### 3. Number and choice of planted points

**Five is a pilot, not a family-wide calibration guarantee.** No universal number of points covers a discontinuous or sharply varying parameter domain.

For development, start with 20 space-filling points plus at least five prespecified stress cases covering boundaries, degenerate behavior, and apparent transitions; retain the existing two cases as regression examples. Add off-grid cases to test search recovery. These numbers are an engineering starting design, not a coverage theorem. Keep a separate concealed evaluation panel after method selection.

Distinguish two outcomes: rejection at the supplied true setting, and failure of the complete search to find any passing setting. For each stated benchmark population, repeat complete trials with independent pseudo-targets and the randomness required by that trial, and report an exact binomial interval. If calibration randomness is shared, disclose the resulting conditioning rather than treating trials as independent unconditional experiments.

For zero errors in K independent trials, the one-sided 95% upper bound is `1 - 0.05^(1/K)`: zero errors in 5 trials permits 45.1%; zero in 64 permits 4.57%; **299 zero-error trials** are needed to push this bound below 1%. This is a precision calculation, not a recommendation to require zero errors from a correctly calibrated 1% test. For nonzero counts use the [Clopper–Pearson construction, available in R's `binom.test`](https://www.stat.math.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/stats/html/binom.test.html).

Sampling parameter points from a declared distribution estimates average performance under that distribution. It does not prove worst-case performance between those points. With the recommended rank test, pointwise validity comes from the simulation design; the planted panel assesses implementation, discrimination, and search behavior.

### 4. False acceptance

**Define the alternative before estimating its pass rate.** “Wrong parameter” is insufficient: distinct settings can induce identical summary distributions. No summary-based test can reliably distinguish them.

Register negative-control sources and tuning budgets. For each control population, run the complete selection and confirmation procedure on independent corpora, recording the event **any candidate survives**. Report that event's frequency and exact interval. Standalone rejection of three default controls does not estimate this search-level quantity.

Use wording such as: “The screen retained x/K corpora from control population Q; its interval is [...]. No false-retention bound is asserted for untested mechanisms.” If an operational benchmark gate is wanted, a concrete initial policy is a **one-sided 95% upper bound at most 5% for each prespecified negative-control population**. Treat 5% as a chosen performance requirement, not a theorem about all alternatives. Specify simultaneous intervals if making a joint confidence claim across populations.

### 5. Multiplicity

**Separate exclusion from positive discovery.** For a fixed finite grid G evaluated completely with valid pointwise tests, define `C = {theta in G: p_theta > alpha}`. If the true setting theta0 is in G, then

`P(C is empty) <= P(p_theta0 <= alpha) <= alpha`.

Consequently, rejecting every setting in that fixed grid does **not** require an automatic Bonferroni division by its number of points. It is an intersection–union argument. This is different from asserting simultaneous correctness of every individual exclusion, which would need appropriate adjustment.

The current adaptive search lacks the premise: it may never evaluate the true setting, and a continuous domain is not a finite grid. A low observed maximum p-value over visited points is not a certified upper bound on the supremum over the domain.

Freeze a candidate list on development data and evaluate every member on confirmation. An empty confirmation set then concerns that list only. If no candidates are forwarded, report a development search failure; do not call it a successful confirmation rejection. Measure full-search miss rates with off-grid plants if making search-performance claims.

Requiring passage on several datasets/statistics can raise the chance of excluding a true setting. Use one primary confirmation criterion, or allocate a total alpha budget across rejection opportunities, or calibrate their joint rule. Alternate transcriptions are correlated views of the same manuscript.

For positive claims, many opportunities to retain a wrong setting remain relevant. A retained set is not a set of discoveries with controlled false discovery rate. Report the search-level negative-control performance described above.

### 6. Refinement stopping

**Replace “stop when the compatible set stops shrinking.”** An empty set at two coarse levels could cause premature stopping, and adding denser points naturally increases the count of compatible neighbors. Counts on different grids do not measure a common region volume.

For the first registered version, use explicit fixed limits: four levels (0–3), shrink factor 0.5, declared integer axes, and a preregistered simulation ceiling sufficient for those levels. Stop earlier only if the next grid contains no new parameter settings, or a registered execution failure prevents completion. Record the stop reason. These limits bound the work; they do not certify resolution of the domain.

The existing single-best-region search can remain if the claim stays restricted to visited settings. Improving search should involve prespecified multiple starting regions or retained basins, evaluated on concealed off-grid plants. Do not equate a noisy local optimum with exclusion of a model family. With a reused seed panel, reevaluating a point is reproducible but supplies no new independent evidence.

### 7. Scale and metric

**Keep weighted z-distance as the initial primary metric; treat it as a discrepancy, not a pivotal z-test.** Calibrate the full scoring algorithm as above. Set `line_len_mean` to weight zero for the reason in section 4B. Retain equal weights on the remaining coordinates for a minimal-change registration only if explicitly accepting that correlated coordinates give some features more influence; they do not represent equal weight to scientific concepts. Treat Mahalanobis as a registered sensitivity analysis until independently tested for power and numerical stability.

The assertion that the scale estimator has been ruled out is too strong. Both resamplers reorder paragraph blocks; subsampling also changes corpus size. Agreement at one planted setting does not validate either estimator for vocabulary, rare-glyph, and recurrence statistics. A finite-population correction derived for a sample mean is not automatically appropriate for every nonlinear statistic here. See [the resampling implementations](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-core/src/lib.rs#L600).

Compute, for every statistic: resample mean minus original value; resample SD; standardized bias; frequency of hitting the scale floor across pseudo-targets; and its contribution to squared distance. Plot or tabulate these alongside between-seed variation across independent targets. For a fixed target and scales,

`E[d^2] = mean_j(Var(V_j)/s_j^2 + (E[V_j]-m_j)^2/s_j^2)`

for equal weights. Large self-distances can therefore reflect resampling bias as well as scale mismatch. Neither 1 nor sqrt(2) is a universal expected median distance.

The committed planted targets floor `h0` at 1e-6; the on-grid target also floors `wlen_10`. Report these explicitly and diagnose their contributions before attributing extreme distances entirely to path dependence. Regularization can reduce correlated weighting, but a 20% smaller self-distance alone does not establish a better test. Compare retention and rejection rates on independent benchmark corpora.

## 3. What the registration must state

Publish one immutable protocol containing:

| Item | Required content |
|---|---|
| Scientific object | Exact implementation, complete parameter defaults and domain, initial state, reset/continuation rules, and whether the domain is continuous or an enumerated list |
| Data and conditioning | Source hashes, parser and normalization, excluded/uncertain glyph handling, partitions, corpus length, imposed layout, and resources learned from each partition |
| Previous exposure | All prior whole-corpus inspection and tuning, current exploratory runs, and which future tests are still genuinely sequestered |
| Fingerprint | Named coordinates, transformations, weights, scales, floors, resampling design, covariance regularization, and a single primary criterion |
| Decision algorithm | N, B or descriptive epsilon procedure, ties, alpha, invalid-output policy, and whether retention means screen passage or statistical non-rejection |
| Search | Initial grid, all fixed defaults, proposals, integer axes, region selection, tie-breaking, levels, work ceiling, seed reuse, unique-point accounting, and stopping conditions |
| Confirmation | Frozen candidate manifest and seeds, confirmation-specific simulation layout and target construction, no tuning after unblinding, and the treatment of no forwarded candidates |
| Calibration | Training versus concealed benchmark cases, parameter sampling distribution, complete-trial definitions, controls, intervals, performance gates, and their scope |
| Multiplicity | Distinct policies for global exclusion, individual exclusions, multiple confirmation gates, and any positive finding claims |
| Reproducibility | Code/build hashes, input manifests, all replicate outputs and failures, unique versus repeated computation, raw ledgers, reference replay, and durable result locations |

Use this negative-result template for the adaptive search:

> Using implementation [hash], data [hash], and registered procedure [ID], we evaluated [P] distinct parameter settings proposed by [search procedure] within [domain], using [budget] simulations. None of the evaluated settings passed [exact criterion] on [specified data]. Unevaluated settings were not excluded. This result concerns the registered summaries conditional on the imposed layout and resources; it neither excludes other implementations of the mechanism nor addresses whether the manuscript carries meaning.

Where only a shortlist was confirmed, replace the middle sentence with “None of the [Q] settings frozen for confirmation was retained on the confirmation evaluation,” and report development failures separately. Add a 1% grid-exclusion interpretation only when the complete fixed-grid and valid-test premises in section 2.5 actually hold.

An incomplete run is “incomplete,” not an empty compatible set. A failed fidelity or positive-control gate is a failed experiment-validation gate, not evidence against the historical hypothesis.

## 4. Additional issues a critical reviewer would raise

**A. Confirmation has prior exposure.** The status log explicitly records five rounds of default tuning against a whole-corpus target before the restricted target was introduced. A later partition does not undo that exposure. Audit its extent and describe confirmation as prospectively frozen evaluation with disclosed previous aggregate exposure, unless a genuinely unexposed source of evidence is available. Whole quires also reduce some leakage risks without establishing independence between parts of one manuscript. See [`SYNTHESIS.md`, draft-1 status](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/docs/SYNTHESIS.md#L212).

**B. Imposed layout is being counted as fit.** The generators receive word counts for every line. Consequently `line_len_mean` matches by construction when the layouts match; it is not independent evidence that a generator explains line structure. Set its primary weight to zero and recalibrate, or retain it only as a layout integrity diagnostic. Claims must be conditional on the supplied layout. Preserve quire/page boundaries and declare how filtering creates joins: the current `Layout` contains line word counts and paragraph flags, while the self-citation generator carries previous-line state through the supplied sequence. See [`Layout`](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-generators/src/lib.rs#L139) and [self-citation generation](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-generators/src/lib.rs#L774).

**C. Calibration and evaluation use mismatched N.** `calibrate` builds epsilon for `grid.replicates` (8 in these reports), then applies it to controls with 16 replicates. `refine` can increase final replicates while retaining one unchanged epsilon. Require matching N or an explicit calibration for each N, layout, and criterion. See [calibration](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-cli/src/main.rs#L956) and [refinement](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-cli/src/refine.rs#L218). The numerical table above shows that this mismatch is material even within the same empirical pool.

**D. Selecting Mahalanobis requires further plumbing.** Although work units support it, the inspected sweep/calibration/refinement paths call the constructor that defaults to z-distance and do not expose the metric choice in their grid/ledger interface. A precision matrix inside the target alone does not switch metrics. Before registering Mahalanobis, thread the selected metric through all those paths and verify the actual distances and recorded identities. See [`sweep::run_grid`](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-cli/src/sweep.rs#L82) and [the constructor default](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-core/src/lib.rs#L547).

**E. Saved reports contradict parts of the narrative.** In both the on-grid and off-grid-refined JSON, rule A's hidden-point result is `compatible: false`, with 6/8 successes and lower bound about 0.409; `CALIBRATION.md` says that it passes or is recovered. The request also swaps the on-grid control labels: the JSON gives bag-of-words 30.97, **gibberish 135.18**, and **Markov 20,647.48**. Regenerate tables from the same versioned reports and resolve the disagreement before registration. These discrepancies do not overturn the stored rule C recoveries. See [on-grid report](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/pipeline/calibration/report-on-grid.json), [refined report](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/pipeline/calibration/report-off-grid-refined.json), and [the request's control table](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/docs/REVIEW-REQUEST-ACCEPTANCE-RULE.md#L34).

**F. Rule B has a symmetry problem in addition to low resolution.** It compares the target to a centroid of N replicates, but each replicate to a centroid of N−1; the target also supplies the metric scales. Those scores are not exchangeable merely because they use a rank formula. If revisiting it, use a fully symmetric construction or an independent reference panel with identically processed targets. The recommendation in section 1 provides the latter. See [`centroid_test`](https://github.com/javidmardanov/VoynichAtHome/blob/f181ceca3b36b2b788f15e445a0df4002009bbc5/kernel/crates/vah-core/src/calib.rs#L124).

**G. Fidelity and specificity are distinct from self-recovery.** Planting and recovering with the same simplified generator proves internal behavior, not fidelity to a published model. Document parameter defaults and deviations, reproduce published examples where possible, and obtain a domain review. Passing the same 30 summaries cannot establish the mechanism's historical identity. Aggregate statistics can also conceal mismatches by Currier stratum or section; register such breakdowns as diagnostics, or calibrate a joint primary criterion if they become rejection gates.

**H. Narrow unsupported introductory claims and document artifacts accurately.** The assertions that no known language/cipher reproduces the statistics and that nobody has tested the full spaces need specified properties and a bounded literature search; they also conflict with the synthesis's withdrawal of universal novelty language. Say which implementations and ranges this study evaluates. The request lists five repetition measures while calling them four. The full ledgers are not committed: `pipeline/calibration/README.md` explains that they must be regenerated. Publish them with the registered experiment and link them directly.
